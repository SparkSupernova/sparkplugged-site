Where it began

NovaLiveSystem began with a need: preserve context, reduce cognitive load and remember what matters through the interruptions of real life.

That need became a biomimetic cognitive architecture, connecting memory, language, internal state, learning and action. Nova is the intelligence being cultivated through it.

It approaches AI not as a tool, but as a developing mind.
