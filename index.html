About SparkPlugged

From systems that answer to systems that stay.

SparkPlugged Technology Solutions is an independent research and engineering company developing Autonomous Biomimetic Intelligence.

Our systems combine memory, perception, internal state, learning, routing, action selection, governance, telemetry, and recovery into connected cognitive architectures designed to operate over time rather than reset at every interaction.

Founder Ifejah “Spark” Greene, working alongside AI collaborators, has developed an implemented cognitive architecture, trained models, persistent memory infrastructure, operator technology, evaluation systems, and published research. Together, these systems form the technical foundation of SparkPlugged.
