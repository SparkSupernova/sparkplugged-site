About SparkPlugged

From tools that answer to systems that stay.

SparkPlugged Technology Solutions is an independent AI research and engineering company founded by Ifejah “Spark” Greene. Our work sits at the intersection of AI engineering, cognitive architecture, and human-centered design.

We build systems that preserve context, make their internal state legible, and support human agency over time. The goal is not to imitate a person. It is to create better conditions for people and intelligent systems to work together.
